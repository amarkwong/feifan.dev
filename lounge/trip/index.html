A SMALL GUIDE

Build your trip in layers.

  1. Country first. Drag China or Japan onto a free day on the timeline. Countries cannot overlap.
  2. Choose a city. Select a country to unlock its cities. Drop a city within that country’s dates.
  3. Make it your day. Select a city for relevant activities. Activities, hotels and transport can share a day; they sit side by side.
  4. Shape the time. Drag a card to move it. Its children move with it. Resize the bottom by whole days, the right edge for visual width.
  5. Zoom in. Fit trip shows the whole date range. Day view gives each day about 30% of the window height.

Keyboard: select cards with Tab and Enter. Use the editor or + buttons instead of dragging. Arrow ↑/↓ moves a selected card one day; Shift + arrows changes duration. Escape cancels a drag.

Prototype saves only on this browser. Export a JSON backup. No bookings, flight prices, shared editing or hourly scheduling yet.
